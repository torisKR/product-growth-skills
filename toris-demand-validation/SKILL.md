# Demand validation before expansion

A working demo, a customer problem, and willingness to pay are different observations. This is an external-research-derived practice, not the owner's proven commercial experience.

## Quick start

1. Read project instructions and [execution defaults](../shared-references/execution-defaults.md). Preserve the selected agent, existing work and closed-project decisions.
2. Read [the playbook](references/playbook.md). Use R01-R05 and source records in the [research bundle](../research/solo-developer-2026-10-15/README.md).
3. State the decision, likely customer, recent problem, existing workaround, reachable channel, cost/capacity ceiling, and evidence available. Unknowns remain unknown.
4. Separate reported pain, observed task success, a concrete commitment, and a verified transaction. Choose the smallest honest experiment that could change the next decision.
5. Execute only the authorized part: inspect supplied research, produce a pilot brief, or implement a bounded local prototype. Interviews, outreach, public pages, charges and paid acquisition need their own authorization.
6. Produce a decision memo with supported, contradicted and unresolved assumptions. Do not manufacture customers, interview quotes, market prevalence, revenue or product-market fit.

## Decision gates

| Evidence available | Appropriate next step | Unsupported conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| AI personas or compliments only | Research plan and neutral interview guide | Validated demand |
| Concrete pain, no delivery observation | Bounded manual pilot or task prototype | Proven product value |
| Task success, no commitment | Test an honestly described offer | Willingness to pay |
| Verified payment, unknown delivery cost | Reconcile costs and delivery burden | Sustainable profit |
| External benchmark only | Generate a local question | Forecast or causal lift |

A failed recruitment attempt may reflect channel access rather than an absent problem. A successful pilot may depend on founder labor. Record both rather than optimizing for a flattering story.

## Required output

Deliver an evidence matrix, one bounded pilot contract, and a continue/change/stop memo. Each claim needs a source class and limitation. Predeclared thresholds are owner-selected decisions, not industry laws. In a plan-only request, label every future activity unexecuted.

## Definition of done

The decision and exclusions are explicit; evidence is linked to its source; the smallest test has a feasible delivery and cost boundary; observed results are separated from proposed work. Local prototype checks are reported separately from user research and business outcomes. No public launch, spend, contact or real transaction occurs implicitly. Return the handoff in the user's language.
